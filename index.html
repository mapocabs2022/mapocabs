More Than Just a Ride. It's Your Journey, Our Responsibility.

mapocabs is a growing cab service built with a simple idea — travel should be comfortable, reliable, and stress-free.

Founded in 2022 in Vapi, mapocabs provides local, outstation, airport, and customized cab services for individuals, families, and businesses. From a quick ride across the city to a long-distance journey, we focus on making every trip smooth from booking to drop-off.

We work with a trusted network of professional drivers and vehicle partners while maintaining our commitment to punctuality, safety, transparent pricing, and customer comfort.

At mapocabs, we believe a cab service is not just about getting you from Point A to Point B. It's about respecting your time, taking care of your journey, and making sure you reach your destination with peace of mind.

Is the fare I see final?

For our fixed-fare routes (airport transfers, Statue of Unity, Trimbakeshwar, local packages and short rides) the fare shown is the fare. For other routes, the fare is calculated automatically from the approximate distance and marked "Estimate" — we confirm the final fare with you on WhatsApp before your trip.

What is not included in the fare?

Toll tax is excluded on outstation and local packages (included on airport transfers). Parking and driver allowance are extra where applicable, and extra kilometres or hours beyond a package are charged at the rates shown in "Extra Charges & Terms" above.

Can you pick me up from another city?

Yes. Choose your pickup city in the "From" box in Plan a trip. If your city isn't in the list, send us a quote request and we'll reply on WhatsApp.

How do I download my bill?

Once your ride is marked complete, open the "My bill" page, enter your Booking ID (from your booking confirmation) and the mobile number you booked with, and download your bill as a PDF.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Message us on WhatsApp with your Booking ID and we'll help you.

How do I pay?

We confirm the payment details with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your booking.

Extra Charges & Terms

Applies if you exceed the included kilometres or hours in a package.
  • Toll tax excluded on outstation & local packages. Included on airport transfers.
  • Parking charges extra, if applicable.
  • Minimum 300 km per day on outstation full-day trips.
  • Driver allowance (D/A) charged extra.
